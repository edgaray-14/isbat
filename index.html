Welcome to the IT Department

The Department of Information Technology at ISBAT University prepares students for a fast-changing digital world. Our lecturers combine academic knowledge with industry experience, so every course connects theory to the problems employers actually need solved.

Students learn in modern computer laboratories, work on practical projects, and graduate with skills in software development, networking, data and cybersecurity. Whether you are starting your career or upgrading it, you will find a place to grow with us.

Programmes Offered

  • Bachelor of Science in Information Technology
  • Bachelor of Science in Computer Science
  • Diploma in Information Technology
  • Certificate in Computer Applications
